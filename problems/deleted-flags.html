Keep manual deletion flags across pose-editing sessions

A researcher deletes an incorrect pose point and saves it. Reopening and saving again can erase the flag that identifies the deletion as a deliberate human correction, even though the coordinates remain missing.

Local candidate passes; upstream work overlaps

Who encounters it and why it matters

Researchers saving and reopening manually corrected movement datasets.

Retain the distinction between a deliberate deletion and a point that was never detected across editing sessions. No measured researcher benefit is claimed.

Science and terms

Animal-pose tracks describe estimated positions over time. A missing coordinate can mean the tracker never detected a point or a person deliberately removed it. Preserving that distinction supports provenance auditing; it does not establish biological correctness.

Provenance
A record of where data or a change came from.
Edit flag
A boolean recording a deliberate correction, rather than the numerical confidence of a model.
Native round trip
Save a constructed dataset as netCDF, reopen it, and pass it through the existing converter again.

Where the problem appears

  1. Load constructed poses

    Four frames, two keypoints and two individuals at 30 Hz.

  2. Remove one point

    Select the intended point through the actual Qt canvas and send the Delete key.

  3. Save and reopen

    Use real Save/Load buttons twice in the rendered replay; separate converter cases cover one, two or four native reopen cycles.

  4. Compare provenance and values

    Check every edit flag, position and confidence, including never-detected controls.

The solution being tested

For points absent from the live napari layer, the candidate preserves loaded edit flags as well as detecting new removals. Points still present use their current live flags. Another contributor reported the same approach first; this is independent local regression evidence, not a novel Kufu design.

Validation and limits

Six baseline deletion regressions fail and all 15 candidate cases pass. The new rendered local replay uses actual Qt selection, Delete, Save and reload: baseline loses the deletion flag after the second save, while the unchanged candidate preserves it through the final loader. Positions/confidence/time and never-detected/unscored controls pass. Existing conversion coverage remains separate.

What remains unverified

New rendered CI integration is pending publication. Full host suite, reset/re-add UI, real recordings, researcher benefit and upstream acceptance remain unverified. Another contributor has reported the same repair approach.

What would count as resolving it

A pinned regression fails before and passes after the fix for deletion-only and mixed edits, without resurrecting coordinates or changing unrelated provenance.
