Kufu Labs

工夫 Kufū · practical ingenuity

Kufu is a Japanese word for devising better ways to do something. We take that as a working principle: understand a real need, make a focused improvement, and check whether it helps.

Meaning of 工夫 in Kanjipedia

Why this lab exists

To build free tools that remove specific obstacles from research work. The aim is to help people spend more time on their questions and less time working around software.

We begin with reported needs and existing tools. A small contribution that helps someone is a worthwhile result.

What we want to contribute

Useful software, readable implementations and reproducible examples that others can inspect, adapt and improve. We keep the original problem and the limits of each result visible, and give existing work its credit.
