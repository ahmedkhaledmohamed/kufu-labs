Assess label-free fluorescence prediction across acquisitions

Fluorescence labelling consumes resources and can perturb cells.

Research only; no Kufu solution implemented

Who encounters it and why it matters

Microscopy researchers comparing predicted fluorescence with measured images.

Determine whether a prediction remains useful for a declared measurement across acquisition conditions; no replacement of physical measurements is established.

Evidence and existing tools

BiaPy Light My Cells models and napari-bioimageio already support prediction.
